About the Artist

Todd “Myz” Myzia

Welcome to Art by Myz

Award-winning Artist and Published Illustrator, Todd Myzia was born in Chicago Illinois and moved to "Colorful" Colorado at the age of twelve.

Todd attended Colorado State University-Pueblo where he majored in Art; he then went on to Pueblo Community College where he could focus specifically on Graphic Design. Currently a freelance artist, Todd has been creating professional art for almost 25 years.

When not putting pencil to paper, or splashing color through a computer screen, he stays busy as a father to four, and being a husband, in his Colorado home
