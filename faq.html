Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take commissions?

Yes. I offer character art, illustrations, and merch-ready designs. Visit the Commissions page for details.

What file formats do you deliver?

Common formats include PNG, JPG, and PDF. Vector or layered files are available for specific projects.

Can I use the artwork commercially?

Standard commissions include personal-use rights. Commercial licensing is available for an additional fee.

How long does a commission take?

Most commissions are completed within 1–3 weeks depending on complexity and current workload.

Do you ship physical items?

Physical merch and apparel are processed through TeePublic or Square. Their shipping and return policies apply.
